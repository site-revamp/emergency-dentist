# 24 Hour Emergency Dentist London – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

24 Hour Emergency Dentist London is a private dental service specialising in emergency dental care across London. The business provides round-the-clock emergency appointments alongside general, cosmetic and specialist dental treatments through two clinic locations: Baker Street Dental and The Whiter Smile.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning the business as an accessible emergency dental service, helping patients quickly understand available treatments, identify clinic locations and contact the practice when urgent dental attention is required.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Emergency Service Visibility

**Previous issue:** Emergency dental services needed clearer organisation and stronger visibility to help patients quickly identify the treatment they require.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Prioritised emergency dentistry within the homepage content structure.
- Organised common emergency treatments into clearer categories.
- Improved the presentation of services such as toothache treatment, dental abscesses, broken teeth and emergency root canal treatment.

**Result:** Patients can identify relevant emergency services more quickly without navigating through unrelated treatment information.

### 2. Stronger Trust and Clinical Credibility

**Previous issue:** The practice's experience, professional expertise and patient confidence indicators needed clearer presentation.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Improved the visibility of the business's emergency dentistry history dating back to 1972.
- Strengthened the presentation of its dental professionals and specialist capabilities.
- Improved the placement of patient reviews and professional recognition.
- Highlighted the availability of two London clinic locations.

**Result:** Visitors receive a clearer understanding of the practice's experience, clinical capabilities and emergency dental service.

### 3. Improved Emergency Contact and Booking Pathways

**Previous issue:** Patients experiencing urgent dental problems need immediate access to contact and booking information.

**Implemented improvement:**
- Increased the prominence of emergency telephone calls to action.
- Improved access to online appointment booking.
- Made clinic location information easier to identify.
- Strengthened navigation towards contact and location details.

**Result:** Patients have clearer pathways to call the practice, arrange appointments and identify the appropriate London clinic.

## 3. Design Improvements

The redesigned homepage introduces the following visual and usability refinements:

- **Visual hierarchy:** Prioritised emergency dental information and important contact actions.
- **Layout:** Improved section organisation and separation of emergency, general and specialist services.
- **Typography:** Refined heading structure and supporting text for easier scanning.
- **Visual presentation:** Improved the arrangement of dental imagery and supporting content.
- **Content consistency:** Created a more organised presentation of treatments, clinical expertise and clinic information.
- **Navigation:** Improved access to emergency services, contact details and clinic locations.
- **User experience:** Structured the homepage around the needs of patients seeking urgent dental assistance.
- **Responsive design:** Adapted the homepage structure for desktop and mobile browsing, supporting access to important contact information across devices.

## 4. Final Outcome

The redesigned 24 Hour Emergency Dentist London homepage provides a clearer and more focused presentation of the business's emergency dental services, clinical expertise and London clinic locations.

The three main improvements focus on emergency service discovery, clinical credibility and faster access to contact and booking options.

The resulting design supports the website's primary objective: helping patients experiencing dental emergencies find relevant treatment information and contact the practice efficiently.
